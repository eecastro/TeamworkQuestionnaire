      How your scores were calculated
      RoleOftenSometimesScoreItems

      Each “often” answer counts 2 points, each “sometimes” counts 1, and each “rarely” counts 0. Each role has 10 items, so the maximum score is 20. Scores of 9 or below (the shaded part of each bar) suggest you are less comfortable in that role.

      Breaking ties: when two or more roles share the highest score, the role with more “often” answers is treated as your stronger tendency, because “often” is the strongest level of agreement. If the roles are still tied after that, you choose which role description sounds more like you.
